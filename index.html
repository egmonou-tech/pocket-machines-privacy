POCKET MACHINES

Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026 · Last updated 7 November 2026

Pocket Machines does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. The app has no server of its own and sends nothing anywhere.

Everything you make in the app — programs, drawings, saved files, creatures, settings — stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Pocket Machines keeps a small amount of information so your machines are as you left them. All of it is written inside the app's own storage area on your device, which other apps cannot read.

Purchases

Pocket Machines offers one optional in-app purchase that unlocks the rest of the machine collection. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

The purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, your name or your Apple Account. The app asks Apple whether this device is entitled to the purchase and records a single yes or no locally. Nothing about your purchase is sent to us or to anyone else.

Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's own privacy policy, not this one. You can read it at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Network use

The app works fully offline. The only time it contacts the network at all is to ask the App Store about the in-app purchase — to fetch the price, to complete a purchase, or to restore a previous one. There is no other network activity of any kind.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics or usage measurement
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt because there is nothing to track
  • No third-party SDKs, frameworks or services
  • No accounts, sign-in or cloud sync
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera
  • No data sold, shared or disclosed to anyone, because none is held

Children

Pocket Machines is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children. It contains no advertising, no social features, no external links out of the app and no user-to-user communication.

Your rights under data protection law

The UK GDPR, the EU General Data Protection Regulation and similar laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act govern the processing of personal data. Those obligations arise when personal data is processed.

Pocket Machines does not collect, process, store or transmit personal data, so no personal data about you exists on our side to access, correct, export, restrict or delete. There is no controller holding a record of you, no lawful basis to declare, no data transferred to another country, and no data retention period, because there is no data.

The information the app saves is held on your own device under your own control. You can erase it at any time from Settings inside the app, or by deleting the app.

If you believe this is inaccurate, or you want to ask anything about it, please get in touch using the address below. If you are in the EU or UK and are not satisfied with the answer, you have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes what it stores or sends, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change. If a change were ever to involve collecting personal data, it would be described here plainly and asked for in the app first.
